# SIOS-ID-001 — Canonical Identification Policy | Frozen v1.0.0
Format: `SIOS-<PREFIX>-<NUMBER>`. Reserved prefixes: MAN, BASELINE, AEA, ABP, ID, GOV, PRI, RM (constitutional); AM, AR, AC, AD, AS (architectural). Three-digit numbering, never reused. Identifiers immutable and independent of version.
