# SIOS-MAN-001 — SIOS Manifest
**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Status:** Frozen | **Classification:** Constitutional Document — Volume I

## Executive Declaration
Knowledge lacks a universally accepted engineering foundation comparable to software/networks/data. SIOS exists to establish one.

## Mission
Establish Knowledge Engineering as a first-class engineering discipline through an open ecosystem of interoperable standards.

## Vision
Knowledge independent of implementation technologies; durable across generations of systems; understandable by humans while processable by intelligent systems; a shared engineering infrastructure serving society.

## Constitutional Commitments
Openness, vendor neutrality, implementation independence, architectural consistency, semantic integrity, transparent governance, backward compatibility, public review, evidence-based evolution, long-term sustainability.

## Success Criteria
Knowledge exchangeable without semantic degradation; common architectural principles adopted; reference implementations demonstrating interoperability; independent community contribution; recognized as a mature discipline; stable foundations inherited by future intelligent systems.

*(Note: this Manifest does not, anywhere, claim SIOS will ground or support real-world decisions — see 02-Research/Audit/ICRAA-Part2.md Deliverable 4 for why this distinction matters.)*
