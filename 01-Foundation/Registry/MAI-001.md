## 3. Core Principle
No artifact may exist outside MAI-001. MAI-001 is the authoritative source of truth.

## 4. Registry Schema (AMENDED)
The registry tables in §5 display a **Summary View** (Artifact ID, Status, Version, Path) for readability. Complete records (Title, Classification, Domain, Collection, Owner, Depends On, Description) are maintained in each artifact's own document header — §5 is an index into that distributed record set, not a duplicate.
