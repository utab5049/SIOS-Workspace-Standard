## 3. ⚠️ Significant Finding — Not Just a Routine Result

While searching for Domain D standard-development sources, this execution located the **actual primary documents** for **RFC 2119** and **RFC 8174** — the two external standards that **SIOS-BASELINE-001's own subordinate specification, CSL-001 (Canonical Specification Language), already cites by name** as normative external references (CSL-001, Appendix C):

> *"RFC 2119 | Requirement Keywords"*
> *"RFC 8174 | Interpretation of Requirement Keywords"*

**The audit finding:** SIOS's constitution has depended on these two documents since its Frozen baseline (2026-07-14), but neither had ever been registered as a Source (SRC) or produced a traceable Evidence record. In other words, SIOS's own normative vocabulary (SHALL/SHOULD/MAY) rests on an external dependency that was never run through SIOS's own evidence pipeline — the exact kind of untracked dependency AERA-001D §10 (Dependency Rules) and MAI-001 §3 (Source of Truth) are designed to prevent.

This is registered here as **SRC-000013 / SRC-000014** — not merely to pad the corpus, but as a corrective action closing a real traceability gap discovered through the pilot itself. This is precisely the kind of finding COR-001's guiding principle (COR-PR-001: "validating the research pipeline, not proving a hypothesis") anticipates: the pipeline found a real gap in SIOS itself, unprompted.

## 6. Known Limitations

1. **RFC 6919** ("Further Key Words for Use in RFCs," 1 April 2013) was also surfaced — a tongue-in-cheek Independent Submission (explicitly an April Fools' Day RFC, "not endorsed by the IETF," no formal standing). It is **excluded** from SRC registration, not because it lacks disagreement value, but because presenting a satirical document as serious methodological critique would itself be a quality violation (ERS-001 quality dimensions would misrepresent Methodological Transparency). Logged here for transparency, not promoted.
2. The GRADE Handbook (gradepro.org) was reviewed but not registered as SRC — it is a living practitioner handbook without a stable academic citation/DOI, better suited as background reading than a traceable Evidence source per QA-02 (Traceability = Missing/Partial).
