# EUM-001 — Evidence Unit Model | Frozen v1.1.0 (amended, §7.3 added)
Types: DEF, CLM, FND, REL, MOD, MTH, FRM, PRN, CRT, GAP, HYP, REC. Support Levels E1(assertion)–E4(multiple independent confirmations).

## §7.3 — Relationship to Observation Types (NEW)
Direct mappings: Definition→DEF, Claim→CLM, Method→MTH, Finding→FND, Limitation→GAP(primary)/CRT(secondary).
Interpretation-only types (REL, MOD, FRM, PRN, CRT, HYP, REC): no fixed Observation-Type origin — arise during Context Analysis/RQ Mapping, from any Observation Type.
**Rule EUM-R-001:** Quality Profile SHALL record direct-vs-interpretation-only in Researcher's Note.
