Architecture: Identity, Source, Research Context, Evidence Content, Classification, Quality Profile, Relationships, Lifecycle, Governance.

## §6.1 — Claim Granularity Principle (NEW)
Confidence/Support Level/Traceability SHALL attach to the smallest independently-assessable sub-claim, not inherit automatically from a parent claim.
**Rule ERS-R-001:** compound claims (e.g., finding + temporal qualifier) — each independently-checkable qualifier carries its own verification basis.
**Rule ERS-R-002:** excerpting/summarizing an Evidence Record for any purpose SHALL preserve each sub-claim's actual evidentiary basis; compressing away a date/source/qualifier while retaining the original confidence label is itself a violation.
*(Origin: directly traceable to a real observed failure — see 02-Research/Audit/Blind-Tests/TEST3-CORRECTION-AND-ERS-RULE.md.)*
