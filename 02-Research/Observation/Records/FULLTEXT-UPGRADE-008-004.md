## SRC-000004 (Aminu, Oyefolahan, Abdullahi, Salaudeen)

**Full author names confirmed:** Enesi Femi Aminu, Ishaq Oyebisi Oyefolahan, Muhammad Bashir Abdullahi, Muhammadu Tajudeen Salaudeen (Federal University of Technology Minna, Nigeria).

### OBSU-000024
**Location:** Abstract, full text (mecs-press.org PDF)
**Statement:** The paper states plainly that no single correct ontology design methodology exists, and classifies existing methodologies along a foundation/derivative axis — e.g., it categorizes the Grüninger-Fox method as a foundational methodology and the FAO-based method as a derivative built on such foundations.
**Type:** Finding | **Confidence:** High

### OBSU-000025
**Location:** §V (Summary and Conclusion), full text
**Statement:** The paper's own proposed hybrid methodology is evaluated against existing methods across three successive analytical passes, using progressively narrower criteria sets (four criteria, then methodology/language pairing, then subject granularity and cross-platform usage) — the paper's stated goal is strengthening ontology development correctness through hybridization rather than proposing a wholly new paradigm.
**Type:** Method | **Confidence:** High

### EV-000011
