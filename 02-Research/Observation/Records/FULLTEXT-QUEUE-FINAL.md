## SRC-000003 — Real Correction Found

**Authors confirmed:** Konstantinos I. Kotis, George A. Vouros, Dimitris Spiliotopoulos. Published in *The Knowledge Engineering Review*, Vol. 35, e4, 2020. DOI: 10.1017/S0269888920000065.

**Correction:** The abstract-level registration (SRC-CORPUS-001-ENTRIES.md) and the resulting EV-000002a both stated a **two-way** Collaborative/Non-Collaborative classification. Fuller search results now confirm the paper actually proposes a **three-way** classification: **Collaborative, Non-Collaborative, and Custom** OEMs, differing in their emphasis on stakeholder involvement. **Confidence:** Medium (unchanged, now more accurately worded)
