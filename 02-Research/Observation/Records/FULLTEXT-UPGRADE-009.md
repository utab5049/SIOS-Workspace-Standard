## EV-000010

**Primary Type:** GAP
**Linked RQ:** RQ-400, RQ-430 (taxonomy/classification)
**Claim:** "As of this survey (data collected through April 2024), no Knowledge Organization System exists that is simultaneously comprehensive, granular, actively maintained, and openly licensed — and seven entire academic disciplines lack any dedicated KOS at all."
**Context Summary:** This is directly relevant to KOM-001's scope. It also offers an interesting structural parallel worth noting explicitly: **SIOS itself, as a knowledge-engineering standard, is attempting to be comprehensive (all domains), and this source's finding suggests that comprehensiveness and maintenance-currency are empirically in tension across every comparable system studied.** This is not a criticism of SIOS — it's a relevant external data point SIOS's own maintainers may want to weigh when deciding how broadly to scope future standardization work (echoing the "over-standardization" risk raised in the earlier strategic discussion this pilot responded to).
**Support Level:** E4 (multiple independent confirmations — the source's own systematic analysis across 45 KOSs, corroborated by direct outreach to domain experts)
**Confidence:** High
**Traceability:** Complete
