## 3. Conceptual Model (Corrected)

### 3.1 Definitional Anchor (EV-000001, revised)

**Claim (confidence: Medium, upgraded from Low-Medium):** An ontology is a specification of a representational vocabulary for a shared domain of discourse (Gruber, 1993).

**Corpus-internal standing:** This is the majority position within the pilot corpus — independently used or cited by SRC-000002 (original), SRC-000016, and SRC-000009 (3 sources). One documented alternative exists: SRC-000005 grounds its own definition in Studer et al. (1998) instead ("a formal, explicit specification of a shared conceptualization"), without citing Gruber. **This alternative is not yet independently registered as its own Source in this corpus** — it is currently known only secondhand, via SRC-000005's citation of it. A future session should register Studer et al. (1998) directly before treating this as a fully resolved two-source comparison.

### 3.2 Stakeholder-Involvement Axis (was: Collaborative/Non-Collaborative; now corrected)

**Claim (confidence: Medium):** Ontology Engineering Methodologies (OEMs) classify along a **three-way** axis by stakeholder-involvement mode: **Collaborative**, **Non-Collaborative**, and **Custom** (SRC-000003), with three associated stakeholder roles feeding into the classification — Domain Experts, Ontology Engineers, Knowledge Workers.

### 3.3 Lineage Axis (new)

**Claim (confidence: Medium):** Independently of §3.2, OEMs also classify along a **Foundation/Derivative** lineage axis (SRC-000004) — e.g., Grüninger-Fox as a foundational method, FAO-based methods as derivative of such foundations.

### 3.4 Trend Observation (unchanged from v0.1)

**Claim (confidence: Low-Medium):** Custom-engineered/Non-Collaborative OEMs remain an active development trend, per a single source's framing (SRC-000003) — still not independently corroborated by a second source.

## 4. Canonical Model (Two-Axis, Corrected)

```
Ontology (§3.1 — majority-position definition, one alternative on record)
   │
   ▼
Ontology Engineering Methodology (OEM)
   │
   ├── Axis 1: Stakeholder Involvement (§3.2)
   │      ├── Collaborative
   │      ├── Non-Collaborative
   │      └── Custom  ──► [Active development trend — §3.4, single-source, weak]
   │
   └── Axis 2: Lineage (§3.3)
          ├── Foundation  (e.g., Grüninger-Fox)
          └── Derivative  (e.g., FAO-based methods)
```

**Note on axis independence:** This corpus does not yet contain evidence on how §3.2 and §3.3 interact (e.g., is a "Custom" OEM more often "Derivative" than "Foundation"?). The two axes are presented as independent because no cross-tabulating source exists yet — not because independence has been established. This should be stated explicitly rather than left implicit, since an implicit assumption of independence is exactly the kind of unstated interpretive step OUM-001's own discipline (Observation ≠ Evidence, "no researcher interpretation" at the extraction stage) is meant to prevent.
